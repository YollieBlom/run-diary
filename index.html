How to export an activity file from your watch or app
Garmin Connect (Web) Open your activity → click the Gear icon (⚙) top-right → select Export File (.FIT) or Export to GPX.
Strava (Web) Open your run → click the three dots (•••) on the left menu → select Export GPX (or Export Original).
COROS App Open the workout summary → tap the Share / Export icon top-right → choose Export Data → select .FIT or .GPX.
Polar Flow (Web) Open your training session → scroll to the bottom → click Export session → choose GPX or TCX.
Suunto / Apple Watch / Samsung Health Open the workout → tap Share / Export (or use HealthFit on iOS / Samsung Health GPX export) to save a .GPX or .FIT file, then upload it above.
